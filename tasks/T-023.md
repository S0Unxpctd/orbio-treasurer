## Goal
The daily post: facts from ledger (book snapshot, leaderboard total, own treasury), copy from treasurer.model('economy'), validated, posted at a fixed Paris hour, logged as a decision.

## In scope
- Leaderboard total fetch (page parser + fixture)
- Prompt template with injected facts

3. Exactly one post per day even if the tick runs 96 times
